# Velship Labs

A design and technology studio in Luanda, Angola. We turn ideas into useful digital solutions for businesses, enterprises and government institutions.

**What we do**
- Websites and web applications
- Mobile applications
- Custom software and internal tools
- AI and automation
- Design and branding
- Care: ongoing support for what we deliver

Our work is senior-led, and anything AI-assisted is reviewed by people. Alongside client services, we also develop products of our own. We work in English and Portuguese, in Angola and worldwide.
